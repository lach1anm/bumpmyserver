This policy explains what data BumpMyServer ("the bot") stores and why.

## Data we store
**About servers that use `/setup`:**
- server ID, chosen bump channel ID and invite channel ID
- your ad: description, category, language, banner URL and colour
- the ad's invite code
- bump history (time, delivery counts) and reminder settings

**About users:**
- the user ID of whoever runs `/bump`, plus their bump points, for leaderboards
- the user ID and reason of anyone who reports an ad

We don't track individual members who join through ads. `/stats` only reads the total use count of the ad invite. We don't read or store message content, and we don't store DMs or personal information beyond Discord IDs.

## How we use it
Only to run the bot: delivering ads, enforcing cooldowns, reminders, stats, leaderboards and moderation. **We never sell or share data** with advertisers, data brokers or anyone else.

## What other servers see
Ads are public. Your server name, icon, member and online counts, ad text, banner and invite are shown in other servers' bump channels.

## The website and dashboard
When you log in with Discord, we ask for the `identify` and `guilds` scopes only. We store:
- your Discord ID, display name and avatar
- the list of servers you can **manage** (servers you're only a member of are discarded straight away)
- your Discord access token, **encrypted**, so we can re-check your permissions. It's deleted and revoked when you log out.

We use one cookie, a session cookie that keeps you logged in (7 days, HttpOnly, Secure). There are no analytics, ads, trackers or third-party scripts on the site.

Servers only appear in the public directory if a server manager turns on **website listing** in the dashboard. It's off by default. Clicks on a listed server's Join button are counted as an anonymous total; nothing about the visitor is stored.

You can download a server's data, delete a server's data, or delete your own data at any time from the dashboard.

## Retention and deletion
Data is kept while the bot is in your server. If you remove the bot, your server stops receiving and sending ads. Login sessions expire after 7 days. You can delete your server's data or your own data instantly from the dashboard, or ask us in the [support server](https://discord.gg/KG2BhHXBDp) and we'll remove it within 30 days.

## Changes
We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
