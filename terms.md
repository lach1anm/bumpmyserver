By adding BumpMyServer ("the bot") to a Discord server or using its commands, you agree to these terms.

## 1. What the bot does
BumpMyServer lets servers advertise to each other. When a server uses `/bump`, its ad is posted in the bump channel of every other server using the bot. By choosing a bump channel in `/setup`, your server agrees to receive ads from other servers in that channel.

## 2. Rules for ads
Your ad and your server must follow [Discord's Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms) and [Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines). In addition, ads must not:
- contain or promote NSFW or age-restricted content
- promote scams, phishing, "free Nitro" giveaways, malware or illegal activity
- include links (your invite is added automatically), mass mentions or hateful content
- impersonate another person, server or brand
- advertise servers that exist mainly to buy, sell or trade Discord accounts, invites or members

## 3. Moderation
We may review, reject or remove any ad, and suspend or ban any server or user from BumpMyServer at any time, with or without notice, especially when an ad is reported. Trying to get around a ban, for example by using alt servers, is not allowed.

## 4. Fair use
Don't abuse the bot: no automating `/bump` with selfbots or scripts, no spamming commands, and no attempts to exploit or overload the service.

The bot is provided free and "as is". We don't guarantee uptime, a particular number of new members, or the quality of the members who join. We aren't responsible for the content of other servers' ads, or for what happens in servers you join through them.

We may update these terms. If you keep using the bot after an update, you accept the new terms.
